The Rubik’s cube and its higher cousins as graphs

Each dot is a sticker and each edge joins two stickers that are neighbours on a slice ring, so every ring is a closed loop of 12 dots. Opposite cells face each other across the circle. Every cell is drawn as a nested grid: a 3×3 block of 3×3 blocks, and so on.
